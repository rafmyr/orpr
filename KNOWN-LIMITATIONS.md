# Known limitations

This document defines the boundary of **ORPR v0.1 — mapa nawigacyjna**.

1. **Navigation, not full definitions.** A process name and technical identifier do not define
   its scope, trigger, outcome, roles, controls or evidence.
2. **No complete flow graph.** The package does not assert a complete set of process-to-process
   handoffs, gates or cross-cutting invocations. Lines discussed in the map remain subject to the
   evidence statuses stated there.
3. **No process cards.** Cards and domain profiles are outside the allowlist, including cards that
   may have an internal released status elsewhere in the repository.
4. **Proposal-derived source.** The map states that its navigation content was transcribed from a
   working index whose rows remain `PROPOSAL`. Approval of the navigation map does not silently
   promote those source rows into complete process definitions.
5. **Notation is not frozen.** The map explicitly marks notation freeze as `PENDING`. Stable
   technical identifiers and current navigation addresses have different change semantics, as
   explained in the map.
6. **One candidate is not part of the 70.** The loyalty-program candidate has neither a sequence
   number nor a stable technical identifier and is excluded from the identified-process count.
7. **Internal references are not packaged.** References from the map to working papers, registers,
   prototypes and decision records are provenance pointers, not included dependencies. They may
   not be resolvable from this archive alone.
8. **Polish source language.** The navigation map is supplied in Polish; no authoritative English
   translation is included.
9. **No regulatory assurance.** The package contains no country-specific legal layer and is not
   legal, tax, accounting or compliance advice.
10. **No implementation warranty.** Organizational boundaries, systems and controls must be
    validated for the intended use. The licenses and their warranty disclaimers apply.
11. **Publication remains separate.** Building or reviewing this archive does not create a tag,
    release, public repository, active project page or endorsement by an adopter.
